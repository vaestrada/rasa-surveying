### Attribution obligations if any of these ship

- **CC0 / Public domain** — no attribution required. The three CLLEX images are
  CC0, which is the ideal case: they show an actual RASA project and can be used
  freely, forever.
- **CC BY / CC BY-SA** — attribution is **required**: author, licence name, and a
  link to the licence. CC BY-SA additionally requires that derivatives be shared
  under the same licence. If any of these survive to launch, they need a credit
  line on the page or in a site-wide credits footer.

**Recommendation:** treat every `wc-*` file as temporary. The CC0 CLLEX shots are
the only ones worth keeping long-term, and even those are weaker than a photo
RASA takes itself on a job they actually ran.

## What is deliberately still a placeholder

Three leadership portraits in 06 and three crew portraits in 05 remain empty
frames with shot notes. This is intentional: putting a stock photograph of a
stranger under a job title at RASA would be fabricating a record of who works
there. Only the President's portrait is real, because only his photo exists.
